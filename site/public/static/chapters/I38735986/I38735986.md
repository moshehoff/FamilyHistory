## Tobl Zitserman Introduction

[Tobl](/profiles/Tobl-Hochman-(Hoffman)-Zitserman) (Tubb'l) was the wife of [Moshe Hoffman](/profiles/Moshe-משה-Hoffman-Hochman) and mother of their children.


For more information about her life, see the chapter about her in [Moshe Hoffman's biography](/profiles/Moshe-משה-Hoffman-Hochman#chapter=07-tobl-zitserman).
